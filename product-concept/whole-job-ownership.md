# Whole-Job Ownership

Codex Small Loop accepts responsibility for an agreed outcome. The user should
not need to decompose the work into agent prompts, create workers, route their
messages, reconcile partial answers, or restart the process after each review.
Those mechanics stay inside the managed trajectory.

Ownership begins after the request, execution profile, plan, authority
boundary, and acceptance evidence are agreed. It ends only when the exact
accepted result is delivered, a consequential decision is returned to the
appropriate authority, or a genuine external blocker makes further progress
impossible. Reporting progress or a recoverable failure does not hand the job
back to the user.

The user-facing Controller preserves accumulated intent and the acceptance bar.
It delegates each coherent Milestone to a fresh Primary, monitors and verifies
each result, and retains responsibility for user-facing delivery across the
complete approved outcome. The internal Milestones keep each implementation
assignment bounded without turning delivery into user approval checkpoints.
Specialized Tasks may implement, review, or clarify a correction, but their
partial responsibilities never replace Controller's responsibility for the
whole result.

Whole-job ownership is sustained by the
[continuous delivery loop](/product-concept/continuous-delivery-loop.md), kept
within [authority-gated autonomy](/product-concept/authority-gated-autonomy.md),
closed by [verified delivery](/product-concept/verified-delivery.md), and made
recoverable to new agents through [easy grounding](/product-concept/easy-grounding.md).
