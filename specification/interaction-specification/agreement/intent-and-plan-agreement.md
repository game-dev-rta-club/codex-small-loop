# Intent And Plan Agreement

The Root investigates before asking. It reads the current project, Work Graph,
relevant history, and available environment evidence, then asks only about a
missing decision that would materially change the intended outcome, acceptance
bar, or authority boundary.

When material ambiguity remains, the interview states a hypothesis and
confidence, asks one question at a time with a guess, and ends with an explicit
restatement of the intended result. The user confirms that restatement before
planning. Discoverable facts, internal implementation details, and harmless
edge cases are not turned into user questions.

The plan translates confirmed intent into:

- the outcome and project surface that will change;
- material constraints and decisions already made;
- the authority boundary for consequential actions;
- the acceptance evidence that will support completion; and
- a Work Graph change only when the maintained production structure itself must
  change.

The user explicitly approves this plan and delegates completion decisions inside
its stated outcome and authority boundary. Work Nodes are routing and knowledge
structures, not task boundaries or a backlog of future work. After agreement,
Controller may divide delivery into internal Milestones without seeking another
approval for that breakdown. For each Milestone it forks a fresh Primary
directly from its complete Controller conversation and sends the approved
outcome together with the current scope and later boundaries.
